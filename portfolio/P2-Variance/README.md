# Infosys FY27 budget versus actual

Open **Variance**, row **11**, in Excel. In Power BI, the Q2 operating-profit variance card shows **753.53**.


| Q2, INR crore | Frozen budget (simulated) | Reported actual |
|---|---:|---:|
| Revenue | 45,602.25 | 49,244.00 |
| Cost of sales | 31,557.60 | 33,943.00 |
| Operating profit | 9,576.47 | 10,330.00 |

The Q2 operating-profit variance was ₹753.53 crore favourable. At the budgeted cost-of-sales ratio, actual revenue implies ₹34,077.75 crore of costs. Actual costs came in ₹134.75 crore below that benchmark.

Open **Variance** and **Bridge** in Excel. The refreshed Desktop report opens with September 2026 selected. The three cards and cost-line table reconcile to Excel. Clear the period selection to see the full trend.
